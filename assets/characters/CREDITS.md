# Kenney RPG Urban Pack

Created by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-urban-pack) and released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

`urban_walk.png` uses 36 frames from the [author's release](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-urban-pack). It repacks three characters from `Tilemap/tilemap_packed.png` into 16×16 cells. Each group has three frames per direction, in the order down, up, left, right. Life Log RPG applies avatar colors and glasses at runtime.
